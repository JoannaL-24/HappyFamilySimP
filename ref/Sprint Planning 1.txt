Happy Family Sim


Sprint 1 Planning Document
Team 21: Joanna Lin, Taylor Huang, William Hou, Zoey Lu
________________


Sprint Overview:


Sprint 1 focuses on establishing the foundational architecture and core loop of the Happy Family Sim application. The primary objective is to implement the basic structures of our codebase, such as setting up the database, user authentication/account creation, and the core of the game renderer. Key deliverables include:
* Infrastructure Setup: Establishing the connection between the Flutter mobile client and the Supabase backend.
* Parent Management Tools: Enabling parents to create custom tasks, assign them to specific children, and manage their family profiles.
* Child Experience: Developing the initial Flame game engine environment where children can view their virtual creatures and track accumulated resource rewards like food and water.
* Game Core: Implementing database synchronization so that task approvals reflect in the child’s inventory and the game environment. Also implementing the game renderer for our game environment.
By the end of this sprint, the team aims to have a functional pipeline where a parent can assign a chore and a child can see their creature within the integrated game engine.
Scrum Master: Joanna Lin


Meeting Schedule: MWF at 7 PM


Risks and Challenges:
* Finding suitable art assets and/or drawing them ourselves
* Learning Flutter, Supabase, and other frameworks since none of us have used them before
* Learning how to write a game because most people in the team have not before.
* Learning how to integrate a game into a mobile app, because our app is not purely game-based nor a standard mobile app.
